Shipping & delivery

VELUNE currently ships within the United States, including Washington, D.C. Orders are processed within 1–2 business days, Monday through Friday, excluding U.S. federal holidays.

  • Standard delivery — $8, arriving in 3–5 business days after processing. Complimentary on merchandise totals of $150 or more after discounts.
  • Express delivery — $18, arriving in 1–2 business days after processing. Complimentary shipping does not apply to express delivery.

Delivery estimates are not guarantees. Weather, carrier delays, incorrect addresses and periods of high volume can extend transit time. We will share tracking information when an order ships.

Please check your address carefully at checkout. If an address is entered incorrectly, contact us as soon as possible at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. Once a parcel has shipped, an address change may not be possible.

Orders are processed by VELUNE and dispatched with a U.S. carrier. A tracking number is added to your order page once the parcel ships. We may contact you by email, phone or WhatsApp to confirm availability and delivery details before dispatch.

Returns & exchanges

Unused pieces in original condition and packaging may be returned within 30 days of delivery. Items should be unworn, unwashed and free of marks, with any tags and protective packaging intact.

How to start a return

  • Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with your order number and the item you would like to return.
  • We will confirm eligibility and provide return instructions within one business day, Monday–Friday.
  • Return shipping is the customer's responsibility unless the item arrived damaged, defective or incorrect. We recommend a tracked service.
  • Refunds are issued within 5–7 business days after the returned item is inspected and approved. For cash-on-delivery orders, the refund is issued to your bank account or as store credit, whichever you prefer.

Exchanges

If you would like a different colour, size or piece, the quickest path is to return the unused item for a refund and place a new order, subject to availability.

Non-returnable items

For hygiene and safety reasons, used personal-care pieces, earrings and opened consumables cannot be returned. Final-sale items are marked at the point of purchase and are not eligible for return unless faulty.

Return instructions and any return label are provided by our support team after eligibility is confirmed. For cash-on-delivery orders, no card payment is ever taken, so there is no card transaction to reverse.

Privacy

This policy explains the information VELUNE collects and how it is used. We aim to collect only what is needed to complete an order and support you afterwards.

Information we collect

  • Order information: name, email address, optional phone number, shipping address, order contents, promo code and any order note you provide.
  • Account information: if you register, your name, email address, optional phone number, a securely hashed password and your marketing preference. We never store your password in plain text.
  • Support information: the email address and message you submit through the contact form or support chat, plus the related order number if you provide one.
  • Marketing information: your email address if you choose to join the newsletter or opt in from your account.
  • Technical information: standard server logs, including IP address, request path, timestamps and error information. IP addresses are also used briefly in memory to rate-limit abusive requests.

How we use information

We use order information to fulfil and communicate about orders, process returns and provide support. We use contact details you submit voluntarily to respond to you. We use technical information to operate, secure and diagnose the service.

Cookies and local storage

This store does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics. Your bag is stored in your browser's local storage so it survives a page refresh. If you sign in, a signed, HttpOnly session cookie keeps you signed in for up to 30 days; signing out clears it. The admin dashboard stores a short-lived session token in session storage. Clearing site data removes local values.

Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information. To fulfil orders we share the minimum necessary with carriers (name, address, phone number and email) and with our email provider so we can answer you. Because payment is collected in person on delivery, no card or bank details are collected, transmitted or stored by this store.

Retention

Order, account and support records are retained for as long as needed to operate the store, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and prevent fraud. Records are stored until you ask for them to be removed or an administrator removes them from the dashboard. Newsletter addresses remain until you ask to be removed.

Your choices and rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete personal information, close your account, or unsubscribe from marketing, by writing to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} or using the contact page. You can also edit your name, phone number and marketing preference from your account. We may need to verify your identity and may retain records where the law requires it. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws.

Security

In a deployed environment, the storefront is intended to run behind HTTPS. Account passwords are hashed with scrypt and never stored in plain text. Customer and admin sessions use signed, expiring tokens; customer cookies are HttpOnly and SameSite. Secrets are supplied through environment variables rather than committed to the repository, and security headers are set on responses. No system is completely secure; please use a unique password for any account you create.

Children

The store is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will remove it.

Accessibility

We want the store to work well with a keyboard, a screen reader and a range of devices. This build includes visible focus states, semantic headings and landmarks, descriptive image alternatives, reduced-motion support, form labels and controls that announce their state.

We are working toward WCAG 2.2 AA but cannot claim full conformance. If something is difficult to use, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with the page and a short description. We will take the feedback seriously and offer help completing an order by email.
